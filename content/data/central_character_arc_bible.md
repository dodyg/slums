# Central-character arc bible

These are design constraints for the five central arcs. They are deliberately separate from
medical diagnosis, hidden trust thresholds, and the player's ending label.

| Character | Voice and history | Material need | Boundary | Relationship callbacks | Month-end states |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mother | concise, observant, protective; a widow who remembers every promise | care, privacy, and a say in household help | will not trade another person's safety for her own comfort | Mona's rota, Salma's care plan | shared care or defended privacy |
| Mona | practical, quick, generous without pretending generosity is free | water, medicine, and a workable building rota | will not hide a shortage that puts children at risk | mother, Mahmoud's ledger | public mutual aid or private reserve |
| Salma | precise, tired, humane; a clinic worker correcting narrow systems by hand | safe medicine storage and patient privacy | will not turn a patient into evidence without consent | mother, crisis appeal | public failure log or protected patient log |
| Hajj Mahmoud | formal, reputation-conscious, capable of changing when shown the cost | accountable roof governance and a solvent building | will not be humiliated for sport | Mona's rota, Umm Karim's warnings | open ledger or private authority |
| Umm Karim | economical, indirect, alert to who can repeat what | warnings that protect sources and reach people in time | will not expose a source to make a point | Salma's evidence, Mahmoud's governance | shared protocol or informal network |

Each arc has five scheduled Ink beats: introduction, transaction, vulnerability, conflict, and
outcome. A refusal records a different decision and keeps the arc moving. The Core state stores
the decision and beat history; affinity is only a supporting signal.

The mother's name and any named diagnosis remain product and cultural-review decisions. Current
content gives her agency and preferences without silently establishing either.
