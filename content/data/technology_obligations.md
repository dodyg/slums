# Technology capability and obligation ledger

Technology is useful only through access, maintenance, people, and institutions. The values below
are gameplay abstractions, not claims about exact 2060 prices.

| System | Capability | Owner/access | Failure or obligation | Low-tech alternative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Repairable handset and wallet | messages, rosters, digital payment | household or neighbor repair network | credit drain and data exposure | paper notes, in-person payment, runners |
| Solar-and-storage microgrid | cooling, pump timing, medicine storage | rooftop cooperative | repair debt, aged storage, public governance | shade, water rota, manual repair, shared cooling |
| Autonomous electric transit | shorter paid travel and bulk movement | mapped corridors, depots, permits | route disruption and permit scrutiny | walking, microbus, trusted driver network |
| Biometrics | faster identity and eligibility checks | state and service kiosks | false exclusion and appeal queue | paper documents, witness, human review |
| Telemedicine | bounded triage and reminders | clinic service with a nurse | patch-reader error and daily access limit | clinic visit, trained neighbor, ordinary medicine |
| Bounded allocation model | classifies infrastructure consumers | cooperative appeal process | incomplete records and visible confidence | ledgers, meter evidence, public meeting |
| Service robots | salvage, triage, assisted transit | household or workshop owner | condition wear, parts, scrutiny, retained route data | human labor and mutual aid |

No system in this ledger is sentient or omniscient. Models are narrow, fallible, and accountable
to the people and institutions that deploy or repair them.
