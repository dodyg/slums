# Rooftop cooperative crisis design sheet

The crisis is a bounded allocation review, not a conscious antagonist.

| Beat | Entry | New information | Approaches | Persistent consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | day 1+ | shared pump, battery, cooling-room rules | inspect ledgers or listen | crisis discovered |
| Classification | day 6+ | irregular-consumer downgrade | gather evidence or accept a narrower share | access pressure |
| Appeal | day 12+ | medicine storage and meter records conflict | clinic evidence, ledger, network warning | evidence/resources |
| Heat emergency | day 18+ | hotter nights make the review immediate | mutual aid, diversion, public pressure | condition, stress, police pressure |
| Commitment | day 25+ | a late strategy closes incompatible options | evidence, mutual aid, diversion, pressure | named crisis decision |
| Resolution | day 30 | the cooperative's answer is concrete but imperfect | protected, shared, restricted, diverted | ending reactivity |

Every decision has a delayed callback three days later. Mixed strategies remain legal until the
commitment beat, and failure endings continue to preempt normal resolution.
