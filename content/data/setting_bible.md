# Cairo 2060 continuity notes

This is a compact continuity reference for authored scenes and review. It does not replace
human Egyptian/Cairene or Sudanese cultural review.

## Geography and institutions

- Imbaba is the home district: dense housing, rooftop water and power cooperation, informal
  markets, neighborhood savings circles, and the central community pressure in the first month.
- Ard al-Liwa is the repair and clinic district. Nurse Salma and Abu Samir's workshop work here.
- Dokki is a wealthier mixed district with formal offices, cafes, mapped autonomous transit, and
  higher surveillance. Youssef's delivery routes cross it.
- Bulaq al-Dakrour is a working-class transport and pharmacy district. Mariam and Safaa work here.
- Shubra is the laundry district. Downtown Cairo is the square and formal checkpoint district.

The city has public agencies, private platforms, neighborhood committees, informal lenders,
repair shops, and faction networks. No automated service is omniscient; records are incomplete,
connectivity fails, and appeals require people, time, and often transport money.

## Climate and adaptation

Cairo remains dry on average, but the longer hot season, hotter nights, heatwaves, water
rotations, power rationing, food spoilage, and rare drain-overwhelming rain shape daily
decisions. Residents maintain shade cloth, reflective roofs, repaired solar storage, cooling
rooms, water rosters, and food cooperatives. These are governed local systems, not effortless
technology.

## Money and language

The player-facing currency label is `LE`. Values are compressed gameplay units representing the
harsh purchasing power of Cairo 2060 without pretending to be a forecast of nominal prices.
In-world dialogue may say "pounds" or use transliterated Arabic, but UI costs and journal
transactions use `LE` consistently.

Arabic appears for clarity and relationship texture: ahwa, taameya, gallabiya, sabr, wallahi,
ya zalameh, ayna, and maghrib are used with surrounding context. Transliteration is not a
dialect caricature. Names and honorifics remain stable across scenes.
